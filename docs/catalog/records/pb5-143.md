## Construction and Hodge data

In the quotient convention, $Y=\mathbb P_{\mathbb P^{5}}(\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(2))$.
A general regular section of $E=\mathcal O_Y(1\xi+1H)\oplus\mathcal O_Y(1\xi+2H)$ defines $X$.
Here $H$ is the pullback of the hyperplane class and $\xi$ the tautological quotient class. The residual is $A=1\xi+1H$; the summands and residual are ample.
