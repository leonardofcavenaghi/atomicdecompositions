## AI Usage Disclaimer, Methodology, and Sources

This website, its source code, catalog generation scripts, and its documentation have been fully generated using **Google Antigravity (AGY)**, an advanced autonomous AI coding assistant. 

### Methodology & Data Authenticity
The process by which this catalog is constructed is completely automated and generated through precise AI instruction:
1. **Mathematical Parsing & Verification:** Artificial Intelligence agents were employed to rigorously parse external mathematical databases. The AI was responsible for cross-referencing descriptions (such as blow-ups or Picard ranks) and deducing which 3-dimensional smooth Fano varieties are realizable as complete intersections in standard or product flag varieties.
2. **Execution of Native Software:** The AI dynamically mapped the identified Fano varieties into their geometric representations (Cartan types, Parabolic nodes, and bundle degrees) and executed the `gwflags` Python library directly. 
3. **Data Authenticity:** The mathematical data presented on this website (such as the quantum connection matrices) is **not hallucinated by the AI**. It is strictly generated by algorithmically extracting output from the native equivariant Atiyah-Bott localization routines implemented in the `gwflags` codebase.
4. **Iterative Auditing:** The AI orchestrated multi-agent interactions (e.g. Proponent, Skeptic, Judge) to debate the mathematical validity of the geometries before inserting them into the catalog.

During the construction of this catalog, the autonomous agents explicitly consulted and extracted foundational descriptions from the following external resources:
- **Fanography.info**: Specifically used as the canonical reference for the 105 deformation families of smooth Fano 3-folds, their Picard ranks, and geometric descriptions (such as blow-ups and hypersurfaces).
