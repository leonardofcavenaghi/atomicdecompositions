    Atomic invariants — points and curves¶

    Atomic invariants · Geometric catalogue · Exact data

    The factor numbers below refer to the characteristic polynomial of A=c1⋆ A = c 1 ⋆ at the stated point. Each row applies separately at every root of its indicated factor. Long exact polynomials and determinants are retained in the downloadable data.

    Construction, inputs and matrix

    Dimension: 1. Ambient rank: 2. Full cohomology rank: 2. Full Hodge-invariant rank: 2.

    Full Hodge polynomial: PX(t)=2 P X ( t ) = 2 .

    Analysis point: y2=1. Unit determinant: nonzero.

    Generality: Smooth regular target under the recorded geometric hypotheses.

    Factor of A A Distinct roots ρα ρ α per root Full rank per root Pα(t) P α ( t ) per root
    1 1 1 1 1 1
    2 1 1 1 1 1

    Hodge identification: All cohomology is of diagonal Hodge type.

    Primitive assignment: Full Tate cohomology; see the common proof rules.

    Exact characteristic factors of A at this point 
    factor 1: lambda - 2; multiplicity per root 1
factor 2: lambda + 2; multiplicity per root 1

    Hodge data: Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch and Koszul calculations, checked at two exact torus points.
