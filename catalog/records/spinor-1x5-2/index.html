    Five hyperplanes and a quadric in the spinor tenfold¶

    Universal catalogue · Fourfolds · Exact data

    Let S=OGr+(5,10)⊂P15 S = OGr + ( 5 , 10 ) ⊂ P 15 be a connected component of the maximal orthogonal Grassmannian, in its half-spinor embedding. A smooth zero locus of OS(1)⊕5⊕OS(2) O S ( 1 ) ⊕ 5 ⊕ O S ( 2 ) is a fourfold with Picard rank one and −KX=h − K X = h . A very general smooth member is irrational.

    Reproduce the calculation¶

    from gwflags import FlagVariety

S = FlagVariety("D5", [4])
K = [[1], [1], [1], [1], [1], [2]]
M, G, indices = S.small_quantum_multiplication(K, workers=0)

    In the flag interface, enter algebra D5, kept roots 4 and Bundle K 1;1;1;1;1;2, then choose Compute quantum matrix. This is a substantial localization calculation; use the checkpoint and progress controls in flag 1.1.0.dev1 or the combined application for a long run.

    basis_indices = [0, 1, 2, 3, 5]
basis_words = [[], [4], [3, 4], [2, 3, 4], [1, 2, 3, 4]]

    The exact matrix uses the returned Schubert basis and the complete automatically supported curve list. It was computed with the existing virtual-localization routines and checked at two independent exact torus evaluations.

    For a very general member, the ambient space is the full rational Hodge-invariant sector. Monodromy acts irreducibly on the vanishing middle cohomology, so first-Chern multiplication acts there by a rational scalar. The ambient characteristic relation annihilates the unit and hence the full quantum ring by associativity. Its only rational root is −10 − 10 ; the vanishing cohomology therefore belongs to that factor. Its Hodge dimensions are 16,111,16 16 , 111 , 16 .
