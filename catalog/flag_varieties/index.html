    Full flag example¶

    The full flag variety Fl(1,2;3) illustrates the untwisted calculation. See Flag varieties and sections for the other computed flag examples.

    For the kept-root beta convention and copyable browser/CLI syntax, see How to Use.

    Reproduce an entry in the software¶

    The full flag card is A2 with kept roots 1,2 and an empty Bundle K field. Use the Space Info action first; it prints the reduced Schubert words that can be copied into a GW-invariant request.
