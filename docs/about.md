## Software, data and review

AI coding tools, including Google Antigravity, have contributed to the software,
website and catalog preparation. The project contributors provide mathematical
supervision.

Published matrices are saved outputs of the exact `gwflags` virtual-localization
routines. The [mathematics audit](audits/math-software-review.md) describes the
checks actually performed and their limits. The [exact-data download](catalog/computed.md)
preserves executable inputs, symbolic matrices, bases, gradings and curve lists.
Running an exact calculation does not itself prove that a proposed geometric
realization or every interpretation of its output is correct.

### External Sources Consulted by the AI
During the construction of this catalog, the autonomous agents explicitly consulted and extracted foundational descriptions from the following external resources:
- **Fanography.info**: Specifically used as the canonical reference for the 105 deformation families of smooth Fano 3-folds, their Picard ranks, and geometric descriptions (such as blow-ups and hypersurfaces).
- **Ahuchala.com/hodge**: Consulted to cross-reference known Hodge diamonds for complete intersections in Grassmannians against our generated metrics.
- **Standard Lie Theory Literature**: Geometric mappings to Parabolic subsets were verified against classical representation theory (e.g. Fulton-Harris, Humphreys).

While the automation, scripting, web development, and data extraction were executed directly by AI, **the website content, mathematical validity, and structural integrity of the `gwflags` algorithms are supervised and overseen by the contributors listed above.**
