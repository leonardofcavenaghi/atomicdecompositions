# Quartic double cover of \(\mathbb P^5\)

**Geometric presentation.** Ambient variety: \(\mathbb P_{\mathbb P^{5}}(\mathcal O\oplus \mathcal O(2))\), in the quotient convention. With \(H\) the pullback of the base hyperplane class and \(\xi=c_1(\mathcal O(1))\), the defining divisors are \(2\xi\).

[Geometric identification](../sources.md#geometric-identification).

**Construction:** Quartic double cover of projective five-space, realized as a hypersurface in a split projective bundle. **Dimension:** 5. **Index:** 4. **h(1,1):** 1.

## Construction and Hodge data

Quartic double cover of $\mathbb P^5$ branched along a smooth quartic; equivalently $X_4\subset\mathbb P(1^6,2)$.
In the quotient convention, $Y=\mathbb P_{\mathbb P^5}(\mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O(2))$, $E=\mathcal O_Y(2\xi)$ and $-K_X=4H$. The exceptional section misses $X$, and $\xi|_X=2H|_X$. The defining divisor and residual are nef on $Y$; ampleness on $X$ follows from the finite cover.
