## Glossary
- **Ambient Space**: The ambient flag variety $G/P$ in which the complete intersection $X$ is embedded.
- **Dimension**: The complex dimension of the resulting geometric space $X$.
- **Fano Index**: The greatest integer $I_X$ dividing the anticanonical class $-K_X$.
- **Basis Rank**: The number of dimensions in the projected flag-ambient cohomology ring $H_{amb}^*(X)$.
- **Quantum Matrix ($y=1$)**: The small quantum multiplication matrix $c_1(TX)\star$.
- **Eigenvalues**: The eigenvalues of the quantum matrix.
- **Hodge Diamond**: The geometric $h^{p,q}$ Hodge numbers of the space.
