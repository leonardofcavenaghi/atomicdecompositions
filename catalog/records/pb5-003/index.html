    PB5-003 --- construction and Hodge data¶

    In the quotient convention, \(Y=\mathbb P_{\mathbb P^{1}}(\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(0))\). A general regular section of \(E=\mathcal O_Y(3\xi+1H)\) defines \(X\). Here \(H\) is the pullback of the hyperplane class and \(\xi\) the tautological quotient class. The residual is \(A=3\xi+1H\); the summands and residual are ample. Workbook source ID: 3.
