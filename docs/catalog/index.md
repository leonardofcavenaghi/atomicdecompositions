# Catalogue of varieties

Browse by **dimension**, then compare construction, Chern index, Hodge data and the ambient quantum operator. A variety may admit several presentations and computation methods. They are recorded as presentations of geometric examples, without treating the input software as a classification of varieties.

## Reading and comparing records

There are **299 saved computation records**, including 168 newly published fourfold and fivefold records. This counts computations and presentations, not distinct isomorphism or deformation classes. Projective spaces, Grassmannians, homogeneous spaces, split projective bundles and their complete intersections can appear in the same dimension list. Repeated presentations have not been identified automatically.

**Recorded Chern index** retains the convention in the source. In the original dataset this is the ambient Chern gcd, which need not equal the intrinsic Fano index: a smooth conic has ambient gcd 1 but geometric index 2. For a point this gcd is not a geometric Fano index. Higher-dimensional source indices retain their stated geometric assumptions; compare them using the [mathematical conventions](../methodology.md) and [fan/divisor conventions](../toric/mathematics.md).

**Ambient matrix rank** is the size of the returned quantum operator, not the full cohomology rank. The full Hodge diamond is given separately when supplied. A dash means that this summary does not assert that datum. Complete-with-notes records have a completed matrix and explicit unresolved connection factors. Unfinished matrices are excluded. A quantum matrix and Hodge data alone do not assign a rationality verdict.

[Download exact datasets](datasets.md). The [Fano threefold classification reference](fanography.md) also includes **Catalog only** families without supported computed matrices. Saved results are validated as records; publication does not repeat every localization computation.
